Privacy Policy

Your gift planning information stays personal.

This Privacy Policy explains how GiftMatch handles information you provide while using the platform.

Information we use

GiftMatch may save your account details, profile preferences, selected interests, wishlist items, saved occasions, reminder settings, and gift recommendation history.

How your information helps you

  • To recommend gifts that match your selected preferences.
  • To save wishlist items and track whether a gift has been purchased.
  • To remind you about upcoming birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and other events.
  • To make your future searches faster and more relevant.

External shopping links

Some gift cards open marketplace pages in a new tab. Those websites have their own privacy practices, so please review their policies before making a purchase.

Your control

You can update your profile, remove wishlist items, edit occasions, and mark notifications as read anytime from your account.
